**Author:** Aman Kumar Singh

## Overview

Personalised clinical report generation for rare neurodevelopmental disorders requires reports that are factually reliable, sufficiently comprehensive, concise, and appropriate for their intended audience. While large language models (LLMs) can support this task, their computational requirements can make deployment challenging in privacy-sensitive clinical settings.

This project investigates **multidimensional quality-aware knowledge distillation** as a means of transferring report-generation capabilities from a large teacher language model to a substantially smaller student model. Rather than treating all teacher-generated responses as equally valuable, the proposed approach uses multiple dimensions of response quality—including **factuality, information coverage, conciseness, and communication quality**—to determine their contribution during training.

**KBG syndrome**, a rare genetic neurodevelopmental disorder, is used as a controlled clinical case study. The resulting compact model is evaluated against conventional **supervised fine-tuning (SFT)**, **Direct Preference Optimisation (DPO)**, and an untrained baseline, considering both **clinical report quality and computational efficiency**.

## Research Question

> Can multidimensional quality-aware knowledge distillation improve the clinical quality of reports generated by compact language models compared with conventional supervised fine-tuning?
